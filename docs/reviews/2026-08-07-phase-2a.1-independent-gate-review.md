## 1. Executive summary

Phase 2A.1 is now committed, pushed, and backed by a fully green remote CI run that executes the complete application pipeline. Every mandatory governance control I was asked to verify is demonstrably enforced by the committed repository, and I confirmed each by planting violations rather than reading assertions.

The two blocking defects from the previous foundation review are closed, and closed properly:

- **Module boundaries now fail CI.** All six dependency-cruiser rules carry `severity: "error"`. I independently planted a violation for each of the five boundary rules plus a circular dependency — **all six exited 1 and named the specific rule**. A committed attack harness (`tests/architecture/attack-boundaries.mjs`) runs in CI and asserts non-zero exit with rule-name matching for three of them.
- **The logger no longer leaks.** Redaction is now value-pattern based as well as key-based, recurses through nested objects and arrays, and covers the `message` parameter. My nine-category attack matrix — including array-of-objects nesting and credential URLs under unknown keys — was redacted 9/9.

Two further improvements arrived unprompted: hard-coded timestamps are gone entirely (`Clock` interface, `SystemClock`, `FixedClock`; zero `new Date(` outside `clock.ts`), and the CI job now runs an explicit integration suite and the boundary attack step.

The residual findings are real but none is a mandatory control failure. The most substantive is that `SourcePreference.mode` is still never read, so the three preference modes are decorative; and mixed-methodology disclosure is not implemented — acceptable only because nothing in the foundation aggregates or averages, so the condition cannot yet arise.

A note on process rather than product: the repository changed under me three times during this review, including Phase 2A.1 landing mid-session. All findings below are pinned to explicit SHAs.

## 12. Clock review

- **Production uses system time** — verified: `systemClock instanceof SystemClock`, and its `now()` is within 5 s of `Date.now()`.
- **Tests use deterministic clocks** — `FixedClock` returns an identical instant on repeated calls and throws on an invalid input.
- **Hard-coded timestamps are gone** — zero occurrences of the previous `"2026-01-01T00:00:00.000Z"` constant anywhere in `src/` or `app/`, and **zero occurrences of `new Date(` outside `clock.ts`**.
- The clock is injected into `log()`, `evaluateCandidate()`, `selectCanonicalSource()` and `createMethodologyBreak()` with `systemClock` as the default.

## 15. Overall decision

**Decision: APPROVED WITH MINOR FINDINGS**

Every mandatory governance control is demonstrably enforced by the committed repository at `0b061406f275ccd5556a27f4c75b16ee5ec96017` and corroborated by verified remote CI evidence in run #9 against that exact SHA.

Specifically, and each established by my own negative testing rather than by reading the implementation's claims: all six architecture rules fail CI when violated; governance denies on both a missing and a denied decision and cannot be reached by any feature flag; every capability flag defaults off while governance, consent, audit and provenance are outside the flag system entirely; the synthetic boundary rejects all seventeen prohibited paths I could construct; no network, OAuth, credential or authentication code exists anywhere; persistence holds only six platform-control entities; the logger redacts across nine attack categories including nested arrays and free-text messages; and time is fully injected with no hard-coded instants remaining.

The findings recorded above are genuine and should be scheduled, but none of them is a mandatory control failure. M-01 and M-02 concern semantics that are not yet reachable in a foundation that selects a single canonical source and never aggregates; M-03 through M-06 are hardening, hygiene and test-coverage items.

**Conditions attached to this approval:**

1. Register the Phase 2A.1 findings and dispositions in `GOV-FIND-001` before the next gate (M-04).
2. Treat M-02 (mixed-methodology disclosure) as a hard blocker for any aggregation, trend or multi-source combination work.
3. Extend the attack harness to all six rules before any further architecture rule is added (M-06).

**Unchanged and still blocking any personal-data work:** C-03 through C-07, together with `P1.1-H-02`, `H-03`, `H-05`, `H-06`, `H-07`, `H-08`, `H-09`, `H-13` and `H-18`. These are not conditions of this approval, which covers synthetic-only foundation work exclusively. Phase 2A.1 authorises nothing beyond that boundary, and this review recommends no implementation beyond it.
